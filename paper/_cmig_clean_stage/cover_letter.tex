\opening{Dear Professor Zhai,}

We transfer the manuscript
``\textbf{Pseudo-3D Heightfield Reconstruction from Endoscopic Video via Normal-Guided Multi-View Optimization and Metric Scale Gauge Injection}''
to \textit{Displays} as a Research Article. The revised manuscript treats the heightfield as a surface that can be displayed and, separately, as a surface whose measurement error is reported.

\textbf{Why Displays.}
The useful output is a renderable mesh for intra-operative overlay and measurement, not a disparity map alone. A collapsed cone or planar slab cannot be visualized as a surgical surface and cannot be used to measure physical lengths. This addresses a fundamental display problem: visual signal processing, an un-collapsed 3D surface, and explicit metric scale recovery.

\textbf{What changed for this transfer.}
\begin{itemize}
    \item \textbf{Normal-guided branch disambiguation:} The heightfield normal selects one of the two branches of a small-baseline homography. On the evaluated pairs the selected translation agrees with the reference at median $|t^\top t_{\mathrm{gt}}| = 0.984$.
    \item \textbf{Multi-stride co-visibility Bundle Adjustment (BA):} We replace sequential dead-reckoning pose chaining with a multi-stride co-visibility graph across non-adjacent keyframes, optimizing $\mathrm{SE}(3)$ Lie-algebra camera poses and triangulated 3D points jointly under a robust Huber loss.
    \item \textbf{Metric scale gauge:} On the 13 real sequences with a visible $3$\,mm board, full-gauge median ATE is $4.49$\,mm (VGGT-1B $11.88$\,mm, ORB-SfM $41.98$\,mm, same 13 sequences). Marker-free BA does not beat VGGT-1B on the 17-sequence median ($29.88$ vs.\ $16.86$\,mm). On \texttt{s10097}, BA is $24.86$\,mm and VGGT-1B is $37.25$\,mm.
    \item \textbf{Stereo validation and baseline gauge:} On public EndoNeRF, the calibrated stereo variant achieves an overall AbsRel of $0.0285$, outperforming FoundationStereo ($0.0321$) and Stereo SGM ($0.0323$). On the chessboard benchmark, sparse gauge triangulation yields a held-out median MAE of $1.45$\,mm, outperforming COLMAP ($3.41$\,mm) and VGGT ($4.65$\,mm).
\end{itemize}

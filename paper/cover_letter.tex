We submit the manuscript
``\textbf{Uncollapsed and Metrically-Scaled 3D Reconstruction from Monocular Endoscopic Video via Pseudo-3D Heightfields}''
to \textit{Results in Engineering} as a Research Article.

\textbf{Engineering problem and fit.}
The work addresses a concrete engineering system problem: how to build a computational pipeline that converts monocular endoscopic video into a three-dimensional surface that is simultaneously \emph{renderable} (un-collapsed, well-formed geometry) and \emph{measurable} (metrically scaled in millimetres). State-of-the-art feed-forward reconstruction models (VGGT, DUSt3R, MASt3R, MUSt3R) collapse into flat slabs or cones and lose metric scale on endoscopic footage, and classical structure-from-motion drifts or mis-decomposes under small planar baselines. Our contribution is an end-to-end geometric signal-processing system whose central object---a bilateral gradient-anchored pseudo-3D heightfield---is engineered to serve two roles at once: a \emph{geometric matching substrate} (frame registration, dense epipolar matching, multi-stride bundle adjustment on $\mathrm{SE}(3)$) and an \emph{operator corrector} (a physical normal field that vetoes the spurious homography branch, and a calibration gauge that injects true metric scale). The system runs on CPU in its classical mode and is validated against nine baselines on four datasets.

\textbf{Headline engineering results.}
\begin{itemize}
    \item \textbf{Un-collapsed geometry:} the only \emph{monocular} reconstruction that stays Normal\,3D on the benchmark ($\lambda_3/\lambda_1 = 0.42$; all feed-forward and SfM baselines $\leq 0.22$).
    \item \textbf{Near-ideal metric scale:} $1.11\times$ recovered scale, versus $3.7\times$--$647\times$ drift for the baselines.
    \item \textbf{Leading depth accuracy:} the calibrated stereo variant attains the best AbsRel ($0.0285$) on the public EndoNeRF benchmark, ahead of FoundationStereo ($0.0321$).
    \item \textbf{Drift suppression:} on a 17-sequence benchmark the pattern-free multi-view BA outperforms ORB-SfM ($35.52$ vs.\ $43.72$\,mm mean ATE) and beats VGGT-1B in a smoke-filled cavity ($24.86$ vs.\ $37.25$\,mm); with the gauge visible it reaches the $4.49$\,mm pattern-localisation floor.
\end{itemize}
